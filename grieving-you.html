Grieving You

Shadow Cartography

I didnt know you took up this much space in my life
I didnt know you held me in place all this time

I met you before i changed
The parts of me evoloved
Except where you resided
The places you and i frequented

Everytime i found the courage to leave
My heart couldnt die
i couldnt return to what i abandoned

I always get stumped
By the soft spot i had for you
It always wore me down
Sacrificing me again & again
To preserve you

When you're so inflexible
And so set in stone
You never could see the truth
In your cemented ways.

A terrorizer wrapped
In all this tenderness
I always end up being swayed
By your "goodness"

As you proudly wear
your victimhood as your armor,
I was stricken by constant guilt,
claiming faults
I shouldn’t have.

Just because i couldn't see
the illusion of the pity
I carried for your sins.

I mistook your suffering
for innocence.
Your softness
for safety.
Your fear
for love.

And so I returned
again and again
to the normal
shaped by your terms

I kept my emotions in the back seat,
making room for every splinter you encountered,
Waking up to every alarm
From your loud nervous system,

I remained frozen
So you could remain in control.

until I was bleeding quietly
so you could remain whole.

I finally woke up from the illusion
I understood
Why i could no longer change

Why parts of me
had evolved
except where you stayed.

And now,
Ive decided to clear space
Im letting the softness i saved for you harden
So i can see you clearly
To finally grieve the places you would never leave.
