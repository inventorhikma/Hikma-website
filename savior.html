Savior

Chaos, My New Order

I can’t do it
Not anymore

I’m tired now
I didn’t realize
It was yet another drug

The conversations
The ones that fueled me
The ones that stimulated my mind

The ones where I state my dominance
Illuminating my savior complex

Showing off my skills
Being the fixer
To your problems
Being useful
To exist

Accepting your ego
Biting the darkness
To show the light

Trying to heal
What you were missing
The blind spots within
Your bruising

It infects my sight
The eyes that used to shoot straight

A blockage
Restricting me
From enjoying my own void

I see the drug
I really do

I get smitten with your soul
As you infect mine with yours
And I ready mine for capture

Believing I’ll be fine
It’s okay
I’ll heal

Once they get it
I’ll go away
I’ll rejuvenate

It’s a faulty game I play
To show you
I can help you
Have you depend on me

Believing I’ll be safe
As I let you
Take from me

It stems from
My desire
For you to validate me

Giving myself the challenge
To fix you
For what I feel is broken
Within you

I could’ve
Loved you as you are
Or left you to be
Just as you are

I’ll just be a guide
I’ll pour into my own journey

I try to play God
And get my own bruising

The healing for myself comes
With a lot of yelling
And begging

It lacks tenderness

After I let your infection
Cover my heart
And cause damage

And if my shoe doesn’t fit
I won’t shapeshift
To make you understand

I’ll go my own way
And find my own healing

I don’t need your validation
I can’t keep up
With this cycle
Of forced giving
