Reflections

Chaos, My New Order

All these poems
I write on manipulations
Schemes, swindles
And tribulations

Pointing at people’s mirrors
Mannerisms
And ways of speaking

How I felt tricked
Betrayed and powerless
My low moments
In my being

They were feelings
In the moment
How I processed
My soul’s progression

They are all reflections
Just as it’s been written
In a wise old expression

What goes around
Comes back around

Energy likes to play
It likes to make effects
Just like dominoes
In a game

Its mirrors do not lie
They entangle
To give a lecture

If you don’t learn
What’s reflected
The cycle will come back
In another structure

The mirrors I speak of
The ones I experienced

If I faced it
Then I possessed it

I attracted
What I messed with

These days
I feel I’m the one
In the poems

Like the ones I described
About the energies
I enticed

Sometimes I’m the doer
I also play the victim

I own the free will
To do either
Every single time
I answered

I bite the energy I attract

Ain’t nobody does
Anything they don’t want

I took it on
All by myself

I only attracted
Who I was

I made the choice
To do it all

I’m the student
And the teacher
Through it all

And so
My karma comes
To me in some way or form

I’ll learn what’s needed
I can’t run at all

The fingers are mirrors
Faces
Reflecting myself

What I am
Or who I was

I’m just seeing
My own reflection
Through my own manipulation

And if you see it reflect
You are just
Seeing yourself
