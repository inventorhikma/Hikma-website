Greatest Khan

Great is an abstract word
It implies something really great though
Someone really great

Great words
Great mind
And a great soulful heart

One so prideful
In her actions
You can’t touch
The sting of her words
And her bashings

Her mind is a bow
So immense in its knowledge
It aims for the bull’s eye
It won’t miss
Any arrow it throws
To make a blow

I hear her cries though
Not based in this reality
But in her energy field
Right through her eyes

She admires chaos and destruction
She bears the weight of it
She says that’s what she is
Disorder at its finest
A satire throne of insanity

She says with me the chaos stops
The stars are alive
The moons shine bright
How could she see me so bright
And herself in such a way

Drowning her tears
In my dismay
But I ask her still
What is it that she wants

Is it the meat that is me
Or the routine
That I offered
To soothe the heart
That she neglected

As she begs
To help my fire
In hopes
That she is useful
And admired

As she hides
From her fears
Aching in her pain
Enduring all the shame
From the lies in her game

Walking on eggshells
In my sight
Trying her best
In her might

She is lost of words
To play with
Running around in circles
She is searching
For a ground to lay in

What is love
For this great soul
Is it the worth found
In the belonging
Or the love found
From being fulfilled

For the Greatest Khan still lives
Her flame still burns
She runs from her own love
But her heart lusts to sing
To fill the void within
