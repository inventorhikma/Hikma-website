Loopholes

Chaos My New Order

I’m swollen,
I’m swell.

Zero or a hundred are my choices—
all of you or nothing at all.

I’m exhausted,
I’m irritated, frustrated.
I’m filled with rage.
I’m torn on what to do.
I’m terrified.

I failed on my assignment,
I gave up on us.

I speculated, deliberated,
tried to find light in us,
tried to force it out of us.

I love and hate loopholes.

I find a loophole to be safe,
as I find a loophole
to cause a wreck.

I swam and I swam
to rob the love you hid from me.

I waited and I waited—
clearly, it’s been years.

I gave and I gave;
I only saved a heavy heart,
which only caused a broken us.

Oh, the loopholes bring me back
to discover what I already found—
lies, tricks, and games.

Disguised in
laughter, lust, and longing
that clearly told me
I created the game—
but only
to hide the pain.
