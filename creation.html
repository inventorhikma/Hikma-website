Creation

Chaos My New Order

Longing for a feeling.
Longing for a dream.
A high like no other.
For a peace of mind

The kind that stays a little longer.

I see a realm of hope and possibility,
For what I dream to create:
A pool of infinite potential,
An order like no other,
Made for those who see the truth.

Breaking off all the shackles,
Surrendering all my resistance,
Laying down all my defenses,
To walk an unknown path.

Accepting all that I do not understand,
Being grateful for what I don't know,
Surrendering what was forced and blind,
Letting go of the weapons
I once used to fight for a lie.

To gain wisdom and faith,
In hopes of becoming worthy to receive more,
To know the true meaning of power within the Self,
To find value only in my self-worth—
Only from my own soul.

Compilations of literary pieces,
Words, scribbles, and voices,
From fragmented thoughts and emotions,
Alongside other creative ventures.

An outlet where
My deepest inner voice speaks.
A space that can carry all of my desires.
A home full of light.
A mansion full of hope.
A world filled with love.

Where doubt, shame, and fear
Are desolate.
Where I am of service
without any weight.

Where stuck energy,
Unwanted weeds,
And bugs
Are transformed—
From their raw, disordered existence in chaos
To a blossoming sacred flower
That springs into light in its purest form.

Creation
