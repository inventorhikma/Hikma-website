The Magician and the Tolerant

Shadow Cartography

You were tolerant of me.
Your great burden of tolerance gave me space,
a witness to my journey,
a listener to my commentary.

Others never stayed long enough
or dealt with me for what I am.
They ran before they could meet my integrity,
but you were okay with the dark magician,
the one that danced in the shadows.

I saw your open well of no boundaries
I went in search of what I couldn’t see in the darkness,
in the black magic I thought was good,
in the many lessons that were shrewd.

My will was stripped long ago,
made small and inadequate,
Shaped by religious indoctrination,
twisted by psychological manipulation,
battered by emotional and physical abuse,
the early stripping of my innocence.

I bent my fire into covert ways
to stop the intrusion,
to survive the habit of being used.

And so I became a reflection,
a mirror that serves
the one that casts judgment.

I let others steer my wheel,
to speak my desires for me.
My magician could open any portal,
But I was programmed to obey

to serve the judgment in the reflection.
To heed to your emotional landscape,
Give the answer it wanted,
the response it demanded

This flaw in my forging
draws me to women whose aspirations are fed with ease,
their desires answered before they’re even named
but only for the ones that can’t hold their own weight.

Your life path exposed you to that burden
young as you were,
no choice but to hold the fire.
Karma, chance, and energy
cast me again as knight in shining armor,
guardian of fragility too delicate to bear itself.

Each loss carved a clearer map of my shadows,
yet the faces returned,
new masks of the same pattern.

I was trained to listen outward,
never inward
serving the master at my steering wheel,
programmed to turn for their needs.

I covert-narcissisted my way through the mindscape,
in the space where I had freedom,
where I knew the grounds best,
realms you never walked,
exposure you never received.

Fire always came to you,
air bent in your favor,
beauty, career, and friendship flocked,
reality obeyed.
You never had to build your own fire.

I was fire without water,
visions without anchor,
dreams without shape.
No wheel to steer,
no hand to manifest.

Connections walk a fragile line
Mine taught me absence,
Yours taught dominion.
Mine left me yearning,
Yours bent the world to your hand.

I knew the role you wanted,
and I performed it.
But if no one teaches limits,
habit turns to entitlement.
People grow convinced they deserve it,
and they get enraged when they lose it.

If you have a genie
who grants infinite sparks,
why would you ever set free
your over-giving wishing tree?

My magic wasn’t bound to three wishes.
It granted infinite servings
with no subscriptions.

The only thing that guarded my soul
was a trace that led to Source itself.
I built pillars and rules to protect myself,
to survive desires that devoured me
but not before setting fire
to the high-maintenance houses
I was summoned to serve.

It’s a twisted tragedy, I’ll say:
a magician who can’t create for herself,
a creator who carries all pain,
a genie trapped by her own giving,
a vessel stopped by her own taking.

You absorbed every encounter,
loved every fracture,
tended to every wound,
sweetened every expression,
I traded harmony for truth.
Your embrace became my suffocation,
while mine ignited combustion.

Your tolerance was the equivalent of my truth,
filled with soft acceptance.
You tolerated the uncontrollable fire
until it burned everything to destruction.

When the one who serves all desires
meets the one who tolerates all shadows,
they drive each other to their limits.
The collision forges a delicate balance:
The server finds her steering wheel.
The tolerant finds her threshold.

And so, when the tower fell,
the server could not exploit.
The tolerant could not be spoiled.
The magician found shelter for her fire,
the tolerant found the spark to raise her walls.

This only came to be
for in this rare convergence,
each loved the other’s essence.
