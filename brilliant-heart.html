Brilliant Heart

Chaos, My New Order

Proud, Patient and Persistent
Is one with such a heart
Compassionate, Kind and Considerate
Even with a heavy heart

The hopeless reality of life
The painful struggles of her past
Doesn’t dim her luminous will
She wears her vulnerable soul
On her sleeve
Realest person you’ll ever meet

Peace, pleasure and passion
Is the make up of her alchemy
And love is her self expression
The joy in my delusion
The truth in my life’s subscription

Melodies, symphonies
And deep deep meanings
You don’t need words
You’ll feel it in her silent presence

In her glowing aura
And in her soulful voice
All you gotta do is listen

I put a dollar on a dream
Best line I ever heard
That’s who she is
She’s a dreamer

A dreamer of what you deem as impossible
But the impossible is definitely possible
For the one with the brilliant heart
Because her existence in this life
Was possible
