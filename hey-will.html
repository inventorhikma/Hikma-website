Hey Will

Chaos, My New Order

Are you mine
To move with
To manifest with
To create with

To decide with
To reject with
To celebrate with
And to flow with

Why are you hidden
Or running
Why you all over the place
Not in one straight line
In one straight path
In one straight thought

Why is your attention in multitudes
Are you afraid, ashamed or doubtful
Or are you stuck in your past

Is it the painful sufferings of your thoughts
Or the ego created by the projected self image
Or is it the lack of trust in the Divine
Isn’t it all just a box

Do you not trust yourself
Do you not believe in your power
In the endless possibility of your energy
In your pure intentions
In the truth of it all
The unexpected ways
The universe can bless you

The past has ended
And the outcomes of the future
Isn’t up to you
Your power is in your actions
Your responses and intentions

You create as you respond
Wouldn’t you rather create something beautiful
With potency in your will and spirit

Careful
What is the story you’re telling yourself
There’s no rush
Take your time
Stay in the room
Slow down, See
Listen
Understand
Discern and respond
Power up and drop a bomb
Be a moving prayer
It’s yours when you’re ready
