Grief

How much must I shed
To be me
How many tears must I cry

How many emotions
Must I parade
For you to see my side

I’ve torn shields
These past few years
To be accepted
By your pride

I won’t back down no more
I’ll sing my heart out
Till you see my stride

All the things I’ve done
So you can see
My cosmic soul

The brave walks I walk
To fulfill my purpose

Will you please stop
Your fighting
And listen to me begging

Telling you I love you
I do not want to lose you
Please release your pride
And see within you

For my culture
It gave me a heavy weight
My discipline and virtue
Bears a heavy burden

For me to walk my truth
On the path
Laid ahead

I’m devoted to my family
I love everyone immensely
I want to be vast and expansive
But only the way I call it

I’ve sifted
Through the lessons
Of my childhood
As you raised me

I can’t follow
The path
You traced

Not for a lie
That bars or constricts me

Not for the sake
Of an old man’s pride

One I see so wise
With so much humility

I learnt so well
Even from afar
I see the diligence
And the bravery

Not to please you

A soulful woman
Who tries her hardest
To be there for me

You taught me so much

How to walk my path
To take nothing
For granted

Even if I don’t make sense
Could you please set me free
To make my own choice
In my destiny

I know my path
It’s written within me

Will you let me walk it
And just be beside me

Nothing is guaranteed
You’ve already seen
With what you planned for me

Just let go of the fear
Of how I’m supposed to be
And your expectations
Of what you want for me

Just see my happiness
In the place
I desire to be

Why can’t you see
The smiles that glimmer
Within me

As I grab fate
By its tails
And follow my own path
With every aspect

My willpower
My never-ending force
It grows with every acceptance

I cannot hide no more
My soul has found its home
I’ve accepted my destiny

Even though
I fear
I have nowhere to go

No family
No acceptance
If I become me

Because I will it
I shoot for the moon

You will take me
As I am

I cannot be
Who you deem me to be

Because I need only
Acceptance from me
To be all
That I want me to be

And so I surrender
To the flow
That runs through me

I take the path of least resistance
I emit only my chi

The full me
For me

From my body
From my mind
From my heart

I burn with desire
To be free

It fell to everyone
Without its full truth

Obeyed its environment
Without its full force

So, I accept myself fully

If the outcome brings
Me losing you

Even though
I love you
So very much

I suppose
It is my reaping
For being true

For I know very well
All desires have a cost

And I choose being true
Even though it costs me you

I want to be like Mulan

Accepted
For accepting herself
And my unique destiny

Shedding her skin
Given to her

As I shed
The skin you gave me

Her wisdom lay within her
Her worth lay within her truth

Her ever-standing soul
That burns with every move

I believe in my path
And it’s wholesome for me

If I choose this road
Could you try to understand

This fight of will and fear
Is an ever-standing battle

Her will won every war
Her truth set her free
From the cage
That barred her chi

Her honor and valor
Is wrapped in unwavering faith

Because of her immensely
Hopeful heart
Respected her make

Now, I leave the decisions
To your will

For you know
Where I stand
In this grief

I’m going to keep walking

I will rise like a phoenix

I hope you hear this earnestly

I won’t ever cry again
Just to be me
