Programmed

I have a beautiful land
Made of gorgeous seas
And scenic mountains

Green, green all around
Numerous formless trees
Infinite in its bound

I have countless wells
Ones I filled with love
Enough to soothe the hearts
Of the wounded
In this world

I cater to my realm
I perceive to tend to it
I water it the way I want
I grow it from my core
To cultivate what I sow

It’s all bright and vivid
I see no one different
Everyone is equal
My streets have no bargains

No fears ride among it
I do not feel any lesser
No one can impose on it
An ego hasn’t formed
No soul is above it

The void is ordinary
Dreams are emissaries
Realities are discoveries
Feelings are temporary
Life is momentary

I haven’t bitten the apple
The dust couldn’t touch me
My vision is still intact
My imagination isn’t impaired
Knowledge hasn’t barred me

My emotions are mine
I cannot hold them back
I listen to my anchor
I speak my heart out
In a dime
I roar with a lion’s pride

Walking in my journey
Knowledge is taunting me
I’m seeing how
I’m “supposed” to be
What’s the ordeal
In this realm of 3D

The dust starts rumbling
Fears surface on my land
I feel them striking me
As they kick me
Off my stride

My hearing is cluttered
My anchor is elusive
It’s been poisoned
By what I’m seeing

Its purpose has been shifted
It’s learning how to leave
The cycles that deceive

My perception is hacked
It’s being programmed
By what I’m seeing

I feel myself stop
I’m holding myself back
My breath is getting shallow
As my emotions start to squeeze

My focus
It’s been swayed
My path is now delayed
Encryption on my realm
I feel it’s been ordained

Now I can be taught
To see things differently
Than what I sought
From the way I was brought
Since my will has been caught

I’m being shown
What is right
Directions of my “path”

In a ruse
To cover up
What I viewed
As my real love

The snake
Tricked me out of my land
My vision has been changed
It has frozen my heart
I entered a new land
As my anchor entered a new cave

I see a realm of conditions
Where I fear my existence
My dreams have been buried
My wants have been scurried

My mountains are covered
Cities built on my grass
Structures over my river
I’ve been eluded
By the mass

I’m looking for a solution
To heal my condition
Finding prescriptions
To unlock layers
Of the encryption

All gifts come
With their own challenge
Eating knowledge’s fruit
Has its own imbalance

I ride the cycles
To find solace

Hoping to come out
On the far end
To my realm of love
And its fountains
