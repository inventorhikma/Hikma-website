Cry Out Happiness

Chaos, My New Order

I cry all the time
I have tears
That reach eternity

From all the pain
I’ve felt
You can see
In all these pages

I’ve cried
To be loved
I’ve cried
For betrayal
And from hurt

I’ve cried
From grief
I’ve cried for
Hope
And I’ve cried
For freedom

But
I want to cry
For happiness
From the feeling
Of euphoria

From bewilderment
Of the feeling
In the moment

From feeling normal
In the present
Being content in the void

To see happy tears
Just out of bliss
For the beauty

Tears of joy
And stillness
I want to cry
For peace and prosperity
