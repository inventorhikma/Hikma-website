The Hardest Thing for Me Was

Shadow Cartography

It’s like your existence,
the kind of person you were,
was a wake-up call.

No matter how much I tried,
we could never find the same page.

Granted, we were enmeshed—
so I couldn’t see it.
But for me,
you represented what I couldn’t accomplish,
because you were so different,
and so was I.

In a way,
I’m proud of us
That we could share a space
Within those differences.

Of course the canvas
I tried to shrink myself for
wouldn’t fit my image,
because we were two full sovereign beings

I think what was painful
was figuring out
that I can be a whole person
without you.

The shared canvas—
however imaginative,
however free—
its limitation
made me think smaller.
It required a dilution
Of my infinite existence

And the separation showed me
that there is a whole other world
that matches my personality.

That you weren’t right,
and I wasn’t right either.
I guess I was happy to be wrong
that I can be more.

Now I see
I carry emotions others don’t,
and I question deeply,
again and again.
