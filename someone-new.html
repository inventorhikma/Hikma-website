Someone New

Shadow Cartography

I'm becoming someone new,
I think I'm excited
to see who that is.

I used travel within
I would Build my own cities
My own streets and gardens
I would Paint my own view of the world

The tools and raw materials
The make of my kingdom
Were rooted in me

But The spectrums are endless,
The ones I can travel with you,
The kind of person I become with you.

I'm plagued by the images of the future,
Or of what my body feels when I think of you.

I've never been good with stories of love,
Or idealization,
Yet I feel them all with you.

The distance between us,
Built from the days we spend apart,
Makes me want to melt when I see you.

Except I'm clearer when I get closer,
More visible.

You wont let me drown
You wont let me melt
You would want me to feel it all,
Right there with you.

Making me want to be more alive,
To take life in with you.

As the days go by,
we build spaces.
Mansions and cities,
Entire worlds within the mind.

As we grow more comfortable,
As safety settles between us,
Something begins to expand.

I feel myself finding space to come out,
Finding protection for my depth,
A voice to speak my desires.

The more space I feel,
The more I build,
The more I show up

You build your city.
I build mine.
You wander your streets,
I wander mine.
And when we meet in the middle,
When our worlds collide

There is something magical in it.
The result of our two kingdoms
Forms a land so wide

Valleys open between the mountains.
Rivers carve through the land.
When your roads meet mine,
Every crossing becomes a new beginning.
Gardens bloom in unexpected places.
New skies stretch above us.

We wander farther than we could alone.
Not because we become one,
But because we remain ourselves,
When we choose to share the view
As a new world forms between us.

When you respond to my sacred heart,
I break open.
I meet parts of myself
I hadn't known before.
I meet myself
As I meet you.

The joyful, the angry,
The wounded child,
The young girl,
The woman,
The wise crone.
Every spectrum
Waiting to be lived.

And I dream to make more,
To build more places we can go,
So we have more places we can become,

All of them built from within us,
Waiting behind unopened doors,
Until we found the courage to enter.
A world neither could build alone.

And so yet again I choose to remain
Even through the pain,
I break open once again.
