Fools

Chaos, My New Order

They respond
With their own will
They act the fool
Stay being the fool

Their intentions
Are not boxed in
It’s mapped out
As they go
As life happens

Life gives
And they respond
They manifest and create
In the scope
Of their own free will

What they want
They get
What they don’t
They release
They’re not attached to anything

Because they’re aware
If they’re detached
They experience life fully

With no excuse or evaluation
Only self actualization
Only for the greater good

Does it add value to my beingness
Or does it infect it
It’s your free will
To decide upon it

It doesn’t matter what happens
You’re a fool anyways
You’ll find a way
For it
