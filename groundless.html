Groundless

Shadow Cartography

I’ve been noticing something
about the energies I entertain.

When I move into a reality,
I first lay down words
then step upon them.

If you don’t give me a ground to walk on,
a truth to talk to,
I will run baseless.

Maybe it’s an ailment,
a little fracture in my making,
something buried deep in my core.

A need for safety attached to truth,
to finding what is real
before I allow myself to belong.

Maybe I’m always searching
for what can bear my weight.

The disconnect is wild.

I learned to see
from the absence of sight.
Because I was made blind.

I was taught a world
that disappeared when I touched it.

Nothing stayed true long enough
for reality to hold.

Every doorway opened
into another mirror.

Cut off from creation,

I built bridges out of patterns
and found ground in language.

If their weight isn’t visible,
I cannot walk on the same ground.

So I keep naming the earth
until I can stand straight.

But we speak different languages.
I tried to learn yours
And I kept falling.

You move faster
than I can become.

If I try to catch up
I’ll outrun myself.

If you can wait,
slow down,
see the way I perceive

Then you’ll find my rhythm.

Give me a little ground.
Give me a little time.

My body will arrive
once the ground forms.

Until then,

I’ll keep placing words,
stepping carefully,
making ground
where none exists.
